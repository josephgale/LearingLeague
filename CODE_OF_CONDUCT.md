# Code of Conduct

## Our Mission

LearingLeague exists to make taxpayer fraud investigation accessible, transparent, and effective. We are a community of journalists, citizens, and developers committed to accountability — not vigilantism.

## Core Rules

### 1. Evidence Only — No Speculation

- Post only what you can document with evidence
- Do not accuse individuals or entities of fraud without supporting data
- "This address is a residential home registered as a daycare" is evidence
- "This person is definitely committing fraud" is speculation — don't post it

### 2. No Doxxing or Harassment

- Do not publish personal information beyond what is in public records
- Do not contact, threaten, or intimidate individuals associated with flagged entities
- Do not share home addresses, phone numbers, or family information of private individuals
- Do not organize or encourage others to confront or harass anyone

### 3. Legal and Ethical Investigation Only

- Do not trespass on private property
- Do not misrepresent yourself (e.g., posing as a government official)
- Follow all applicable local, state, and federal laws
- Photograph only from public spaces

### 4. Honest Reporting

- Do not fabricate or alter evidence
- Do not inflate or manipulate findings for points or recognition
- If you make an error, correct it promptly
- Mark entities as "Seems Legit" when your investigation finds no issues — that's valuable data too

### 5. Respect the Community

- Be civil in all interactions
- Help new investigators learn proper techniques
- Give credit where due
- Compete on the leaderboard with integrity

## Anti-Cheat

- All evidence must be geo-tagged and timestamped
- Leaderboard entries are subject to manual review
- Fabricated evidence results in permanent ban
- Multiple accounts for XP farming result in permanent ban

## Reporting Violations

If you see a violation of this code of conduct, report it to the moderation team. We take violations seriously.

## Enforcement

- **First offense:** Warning and content removal
- **Second offense:** Temporary suspension (7-30 days)
- **Third offense or severe violation:** Permanent ban
- **Fabricated evidence or doxxing:** Immediate permanent ban

## Remember

We are not law enforcement. We are not judges. We are investigators who gather evidence and report it through proper channels. The legal system determines guilt — we just make sure the right questions get asked.
